Welcome to Grafana Cloud

Our mission is to empower you to unite all your data — no matter where it lives — so your teams can analyze, take action, and make smart decisions. Here’s your action plan to get set up right from the start.

Get your organization ready

  • Identify project stakeholders to involve with the Grafana team
  • Designate your organization's Grafana administrators
  • Ensure that admins are invited to the Grafana organization

Decisions to make up front

Set these once, then forget. The strategy and how-to for each lives in your Onboarding Resources.

  • Billing safeguards — designate a cost/FinOps owner, learn your committed budget & how products draw it down, and set usage alerts. Cost management →
  • Stack rollout — decide single- vs multi-stack and which region(s) to deploy in (mind any Private Link needs). Stack considerations →
  • Access & governance — configure SSO, and plan your roles/teams (RBAC) & data access (LBAC). Access & governance →
