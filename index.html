Your onboarding checklist

  • Identify project stakeholders to involve with the Grafana team
  • Designate your organization's Grafana administrators
  • Ensure that admins are invited to the Grafana organization
  • Schedule a kick-off call with your Account Executive

Your first steps in Grafana Cloud as an admin

1
Log in or sign up
Head to grafana.com — or create an account.
2
Select your organization
Choose it from the top-left dropdown list.
3
Deploy your stack
Create a Cloud stack in your region of choice.

How to contact Technical Support

1
Log in & select your org
Sign in at grafana.com and choose your organization.
2
Open the support area
In the admin area, go to SUPPORT > Get Support.
3
Open a ticket
Select Support ticket > + Open a ticket.
Best practices for faster resolution
  • Check the System Status Page first
  • Share versions, logs, config files & screenshots
  • Give detailed steps to reproduce the issue
  • Watch for follow-up questions
