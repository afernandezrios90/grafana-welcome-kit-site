Stack deployment considerations

Region selection strategy

Choose the region closest to your data sources and users to reduce latency and egress. A stack’s region can’t be changed after it’s created, so decide deliberately. To query data behind your firewall, connect it privately with Private Data Source Connect instead of exposing it to the internet.

Regional availability →   Private Data Source Connect →
Single- vs multi-stack

A single stack is simplest and keeps all your telemetry together. Reach for multiple stacks to separate environments (prod vs. non-prod), business units, or data-residency boundaries — accepting the extra administration that comes with it.

RBAC strategy

Role-based access control governs what users can do. Map teams to roles (Viewer/Editor/Admin or custom) for least-privilege access to dashboards, alerts and settings.

Role-based access control →
LBAC strategy

Label-based access control governs what data a team can see. Filter queries by label (team, environment, namespace) so groups sharing a Loki or Prometheus source only see their own telemetry.

Optimize telemetry ingestion

Most cost is created at the source. Trim unused metrics and high-cardinality labels, and let Adaptive Metrics aggregate series you don’t actively query — usually cutting metrics cost without losing visibility.
